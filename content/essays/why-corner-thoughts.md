summary: A short note on what this site is for, and a sample of how essays look here.

Every city has corners where people stop and talk: a kiosk, a matatu stage, the bench outside a bank. Most of what I've learned about how money actually works in Kenya came from listening at corners like these, long before it came from a textbook.

This site is my corner. I'll write about what I notice, and I'd like to hear what you notice too.

## What to expect

- Essays on money and finance in East Africa
- Everyday economics: prices, ads, queues, and the incentives behind them
- The occasional personal piece
